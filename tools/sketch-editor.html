Sketch Editor

Two ways to draw: Click points — click to place a point at a time, straight lines connect them with corners rounded automatically. Draw freehand — click and drag to sketch a stroke in one motion; it's simplified down to a handful of points automatically when you release. The line starts and ends exactly where you click — nothing is added before your first point or after your last one. Drag a point to move it, double-click to delete it. Load a reference photo to trace over it. When you're happy, copy the generated sketch: {...} block into a work in data/works.js. Pick a work from the Reference menu and its main home-page image loads straight from data/works.js, framed in the same box the home page gives it — a small card, at the size the sketch will be seen at.

The box is the home page's card box (1 : 1.08) and the photo is fitted into it the same way — whole, never cropped. The dashed square is the sketch's own 400×400 area: draw inside it, since anything in the strips above or below it gets clipped on the home page. The box is the card's real width, and the line is the weight the feed draws it at, so what is here is what lands there. Aim for roughly the same overall height as the existing sketches (they fill most of the square). Keep the shape as ONE continuous route — don't lift the pen; if you need an interior detail (like a handle or a slat), route the click path out to it and back rather than starting a new shape.
