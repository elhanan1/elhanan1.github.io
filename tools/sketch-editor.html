Sketch Editor

Two ways to draw: Click points — click to place a point at a time, straight lines connect them with corners rounded automatically. Draw freehand — click and drag to sketch a stroke in one motion; it's simplified down to a handful of points automatically when you release. The line starts and ends exactly where you click — nothing is added before your first point or after your last one. Drag a point to move it, double-click to delete it. Load a reference photo to trace over it. When you're happy, copy the generated sketch: {...} block into a work in data/works.js.

Tip: aim for roughly the same overall height as the existing sketches (they fill most of the 400×400 box). Keep the shape as ONE continuous route — don't lift the pen; if you need an interior detail (like a handle or a slat), route the click path out to it and back rather than starting a new shape.
